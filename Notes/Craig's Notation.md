# Craig's Notation

[[Frames]] are notated with curly braces around a capital letter, eg. $\set A$.

$$^A\hat{X}_B$$

This is the notation for the x-axis of $\set B$ as seen from $\set A$.


### Translation

$$^AP_2 =\ ^AP_1 +\ ^AQ$$

Here $^AQ$ is the translation.
