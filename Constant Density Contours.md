# Constant Density Contours
"Slice" a pdf, to represent a distribution in a lower dimension.

### For Normal Distributions
$$
(x-\mu)^{T} \Sigma^{-1}(x-\mu ) = \mathrm{constant} = c^{2}
$$

Quadratic form -> elipsoids (hyperelipsoids in more dimensions)
- Centered at $x=mu$
- Axis direction: eigenvectors $e_{i}$ of $\Sigma$
- Axis halflengths: $c\sqrt{\lambda_{i}}$, where $\lambda_{i}$ are the eigenvalues of $\Sigma$
